## Scope

- `site/failure.html` is a plain HTML page. To be valid and conventional it carries a doctype, `<html lang="en">`, a charset meta, a title "Strikes, timeouts and failure", the stylesheet link exactly as `check.mjs` expects, a `<main>` wrapper with one `<h1>`, and a nav list linking `index.html` ("Home") and `failure.html` ("Failure"), matching `site/index.html`. These are not unrequested.
- The body has five sections with `<h2>` headings: Capacity, Timeouts, Two kinds of failure reason, Retries, Strikes. Prose from the issue is split into `<p>` elements; the reasons may be `<ul>` lists. The Strikes section has a `<table>` with the header cells Failed at, Strikes, Meaning and the five rows from the issue, then the paragraph beginning "Strikes add up".
- `site/index.html` gets exactly one added list item linking `failure.html` as "Failure" in the existing nav. Its position is not constrained.

## Constraints
